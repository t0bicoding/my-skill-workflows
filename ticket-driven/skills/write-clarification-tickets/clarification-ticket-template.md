settle this ticket themselves — the skill decides it instead while drafting tickets, and records that
call in the Decisions section below.

## What to clarify

A brief description of what questions to clarify with this ticket. For a brainstorming ticket, one
entry per point — the question, why it is open, what it blocks — as seeds for the agenda.

## Context / Options

Background needed to understand the question, and the options being weighed (if any).

## Decisions

List of all decisions made after finishing the ticket.

## Findings

Writes important and valuable findings down of a prototype or a websearch or of the codebase.

## Further Notes

Any further notes about the Ticket.
